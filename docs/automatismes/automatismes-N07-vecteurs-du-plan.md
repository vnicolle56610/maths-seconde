## Série A — Réactivation : coordonnées et calculs

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 6 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Répondre rapidement. Les coordonnées sont données dans un repère orthonormé.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Calculer les coordonnées de <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}\)</span>, avec <span class="arithmatex">\(A(1\,;\,2)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(B(4\,;\,-1)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="2">Avec <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3\\-2\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec v\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-1\\5\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span>, calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u+\vec v\)</span>.</li>
<li value="3">Avec <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u\left(\begin{smallmatrix}4\\-1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span>, calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(2\vec u\)</span>.</li>
<li value="4">Déterminer le milieu de <span class="arithmatex">\(A(-2\,;\,5)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(B(4\,;\,1)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="5">Calculer la norme du vecteur <span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3\\4\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="6">Calculer les coordonnées de <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{CD}\)</span>, avec <span class="arithmatex">\(C(1\,;\,0)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(D(-2\,;\,5)\)</span>.</li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Identifier la forme avant de calculer.</li><li>Écrire seulement les étapes utiles.</li><li>Contrôler le signe et les unités.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}=B-A\)</span>, dans cet ordre.</li><li>La norme utilise une racine carrée.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série A"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}4-1\\-1-2\end{smallmatrix}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3\\-3\end{smallmatrix}\right)}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u+\vec v=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2\\3\end{smallmatrix}\right)}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\(2\vec u=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}8\\-2\end{smallmatrix}\right)}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\dfrac{-2+4}{2}\,;\,\dfrac{5+1}{2}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{(1\,;\,3)}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\sqrt{25}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{5}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{CD}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-2-1\\5-0\end{smallmatrix}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-3\\5\end{smallmatrix}\right)}\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> Pour un vecteur <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}\)</span>, on soustrait les coordonnées du point de départ à celles du point d'arrivée.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Attention : <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{BA}\)</span> sont opposés.</div>

## Série B — Vecteurs : Chasles et translations

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 6 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Compléter ou calculer sans figure quand c’est possible.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Compléter : <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{BC}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\ldots\)</span></li>
<li value="2">Écrire un vecteur égal à <span class="arithmatex">\(-\overrightarrow{BA}\)</span>.</li>
<li value="3">Dans un parallélogramme <span class="arithmatex">\(ABCD\)</span>, citer un vecteur égal à <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}\)</span>.</li>
<li value="4">Avec <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2\\-3\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec v\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-5\\1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span>, calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u-\vec v\)</span>.</li>
<li value="5">Le point <span class="arithmatex">\(A(2\,;\,-1)\)</span> est translaté par <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3\\4\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span>. Déterminer <span class="arithmatex">\(A'\)</span>.</li>
<li value="6">Tester si <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{CD}\)</span> avec <span class="arithmatex">\(A(1\,;\,2)\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(B(4\,;\,5)\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(C(-2\,;\,1)\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(D(1\,;\,4)\)</span>.</li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Utiliser la relation de Chasles.</li><li>Une translation ajoute les coordonnées du vecteur.</li><li>Comparer deux vecteurs par leurs coordonnées.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Le sens du vecteur compte.</li><li>Dans un parallélogramme, respecter l'ordre des lettres.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série B"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{BC}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\overrightarrow{AC}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(-\overrightarrow{BA}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\overrightarrow{AB}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\overrightarrow{DC}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u-\vec v\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2-(-5)\\-3-1\end{smallmatrix}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}7\\-4\end{smallmatrix}\right)}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(A'(2+3\,;\,-1+4)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{(5\,;\,3)}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3\\3\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{CD}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3\\3\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\text{oui}}\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> La relation de Chasles permet de raccourcir une somme de vecteurs en suivant un chemin.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Ne pas écrire <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{CD}\)</span> dans un parallélogramme <span class="arithmatex">\(ABCD\)</span> : c'est <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{DC}\)</span>.</div>

## Série C — Colinéarité et alignement

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 7 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Calculer un déterminant ou reconnaître une situation de colinéarité.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Calculer le déterminant de <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2\\3\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec v\left(\begin{smallmatrix}4\\6\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="2">Les vecteurs <span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3\\-2\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-6\\4\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> sont-ils colinéaires ?</li>
<li value="3">Les points <span class="arithmatex">\(A(1\,;\,2)\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(B(5\,;\,4)\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(C(7\,;\,5)\)</span> sont-ils alignés ?</li>
<li value="4">Déterminer <span class="arithmatex">\(k\)</span> pour que <span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2\\k\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}6\\9\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> soient colinéaires.</li>
<li value="5">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(AB\)</span>, avec <span class="arithmatex">\(A(-1\,;\,3)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(B(2\,;\,-1)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="6">Vrai ou faux : si <span class="arithmatex">\(\det(\vec u,\vec v)=0\)</span>, alors <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec v\)</span> sont colinéaires.</li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Pour <span class="arithmatex">\((x;y)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\((x';y')\)</span>, calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(xy'-yx'\)</span>.</li><li>Alignement : comparer deux vecteurs issus d'un même point.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Ne pas confondre colinéarité et égalité.</li><li>Un déterminant nul ne donne pas forcément deux vecteurs égaux.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série C"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(2\times6-3\times4=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(12-12=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{0}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(\det=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(3\times4-(-2)\times(-6)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(12-12=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(0\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\text{oui}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}4\\2\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AC}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}6\\3\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\det=0\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\text{oui}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(2\times9-k\times6=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(0\Rightarrow 18-6k=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(0\Rightarrow \boxed{k=3}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(AB=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\sqrt{25}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{5}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\text{vrai}}\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> Le déterminant nul est le test rapide de colinéarité en coordonnées.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Pour tester l'alignement de trois points, former deux vecteurs avec un point commun.</div>

## Série D — Type épreuve anticipée : QCM

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 5 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Choisir une seule réponse. Aucune justification n’est demandée.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Avec <span class="arithmatex">\(A(-1\,;\,4)\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(B(2\,;\,0)\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}\)</span> vaut :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3\\-4\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-3\\4\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1\\4\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2\\-1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="2">Le milieu de <span class="arithmatex">\(A(0\,;\,6)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(B(4\,;\,2)\)</span> est :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\((4\,;\,8)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\((2\,;\,4)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\((2\,;\,2)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\((1\,;\,3)\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="3">Si <span class="arithmatex">\(\det(\vec u,\vec v)=0\)</span>, alors :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u=\vec v\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u\perp\vec v\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u,\vec v\)</span> colinéaires</li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u+\vec v=\vec 0\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{CA}\)</span> est égal à :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AC}\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{CB}\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{BA}\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{BC}\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="5">Si <span class="arithmatex">\(A(1\,;\,2)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{AB}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-3\\5\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span>, alors <span class="arithmatex">\(B\)</span> est :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\((-2\,;\,7)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\((4\,;\,-3)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\((-3\,;\,5)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\((2\,;\,1)\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="6">La norme de <span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-5\\12\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span> vaut :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(7\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(13\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(17\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\sqrt{119}\)</span></li></ol></li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Lire l'ordre des points.</li><li>Éliminer les réponses impossibles.</li><li>Contrôler les signes.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Le QCM piège souvent le sens des vecteurs.</li><li>Une norme est positive.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série D"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse A</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3\\-4\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)</span></li>
    <li value="2"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse B</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\((2\,;\,4)\)</span></li>
    <li value="3"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse C</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\vec u,\vec v\)</span> colinéaires</li>
    <li value="4"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse B</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\overrightarrow{CB}\)</span></li>
    <li value="5"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse A</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\((-2\,;\,7)\)</span></li>
    <li value="6"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse B</strong> : car <span class="arithmatex">\(25+144=169\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> En QCM, commencer par vérifier le sens et l'ordre des points avant de calculer.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Une réponse qui a le bon calcul mais le sens opposé est fausse.</div>
